Welcome to Laburnum Cottage

Everything you need, and a proper breakfast to start the day.

Laburnum Cottage is a guest house in Mobberley, on Knutsford Road, with off-street parking and a garden for guests to use.

Every room is warm and comfortable all year round, and decorated in tasteful, coordinating styles. Stay a night on the way through, or make it a base for Tatton Park, Knutsford and the rest of Cheshire.
